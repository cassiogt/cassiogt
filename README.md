## What I work on

- **Platform engineering:** an internal developer platform with shared Helm charts, canary releases, self-hosted CI runners and environment promotion.
- **Kubernetes on AWS:** multi-account Terraform, EKS, GitOps with ArgoCD, Karpenter autoscaling.
- **Reliability:** on-call load, alert quality and capacity for peak events. We ran the 2026 FIFA World Cup with zero customer-facing outages.
- **Supply chain security:** secret scanning, immutable releases and signed-image enforcement with Kyverno.
- **Identity:** OAuth2/OIDC on Keycloak, with tokens kept out of the browser.
- **FinOps:** cost targets, savings and reporting the team can act on.
